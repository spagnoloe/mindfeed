# Mindfeed

A knowledge-discovery app where people discover, collect and revisit short Ideas drawn from Books.

## Content

**Idea**:
A short, self-contained piece of knowledge with a title and a brief explanation. Each Idea comes from exactly one Book and has exactly one Topic.
_Avoid_: Card, insight, note, post

**Book**:
The work an Idea was drawn from. Currently the only kind of source; articles, talks, speeches and movies are expected later.
_Avoid_: Source (until a second kind exists), title

**Topic**:
A subject an Idea is about. A Topic belongs to Ideas, not to Books: one Book's Ideas can span several Topics.
_Avoid_: Category, tag, genre

**Trending topics**:
A highlighted selection of Topics shown in Explore.
_Avoid_: Featured topics

## The user's knowledge

**Interests**:
The Topics a user has picked (at least three) to shape their Home feed.
_Avoid_: Preferences, subscriptions, followed topics

**Home feed**:
An endless sequence of recommended Ideas, with those matching the user's Interests first.
_Avoid_: Timeline, stream

**Like**:
A user's mark on an Idea that puts it in their Liked Ideas. Independent of Stacks.
_Avoid_: Save, bookmark, favourite

**Liked Ideas**:
All Ideas a user has liked.
_Avoid_: Saved ideas, favourites

**Stack**:
A user-created, named collection of Ideas with an optional description. An Idea can be in many Stacks.
_Avoid_: Folder, playlist, collection, list

**Library**:
The place holding a user's Liked Ideas and Stacks, and nothing else.
_Avoid_: Saved, collection
